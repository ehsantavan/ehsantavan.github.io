Research

The demo above is a toy version of the question behind my work: how can one model become a different, better assistant for every person, by learning from their history over time?

Personalized agents

Agents built on large language models that build a picture of each user from their interaction history and use it to act on their behalf.

Continual learning

Learning from a stream of new experiences without forgetting what came before, so an agent keeps getting better the longer you use it.

Efficient adaptation

Parameter-efficient fine-tuning such as LoRA, which makes it affordable to adapt a large model to a single user or a new task.

I am a PhD student in the Italian National PhD Program in Artificial Intelligence for Society at the University of Pisa, supervised by Prof. Vincenzo Lomonaco in the ATLAS Lab.

Before starting my PhD, I spent five and a half years as a machine learning and NLP researcher at Part AI Research Center in Tehran, building and adapting language models for real applications.

Publications

  1. Preprint, 2026

    Winter Is Over for Continual Learning: From Continual Representation Learning to Continual Learning Agents

    L. Quarantiello, L. Li, E. Tavan, I. Testa, G. Carfì, G. Gramaglia, J. Bell, D. Malitesta, P. Averty, E. N. Coleman, V. Lomonaco

    Preprints.org, July 2026

    Summary

    A position paper on why continual learning matters again in the era of large models. It traces the field from early ideas of machines that learn over time, through deep continual learning, to Continual Learning Agents: agentic systems designed from the start to adapt and consolidate knowledge as their environment changes.

  2. Journal, 2026

    PEFT unlocked: methodologies, formulas, and applications in efficient LLM adaptation with a specific focus on LoRA

    M. Najafi, E. Tavan, S. Kuhn, S. Alakkari, S. Colreavy

    Artificial Intelligence Review, Springer, July 2026

    Summary

    A comprehensive review of parameter-efficient fine-tuning for large language models, tracing parameter optimization from early methods to today's techniques, with an in-depth look at LoRA and its extensions: their formulations, trade-offs, and applications for adapting LLMs on resource-limited hardware.
